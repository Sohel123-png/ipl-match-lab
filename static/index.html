Know what this model can — and can’t — do

The supplied dataset covers IPL seasons from 2008–2019. This is a learning project, not a live-match forecasting system. It does not include current squads, player availability, pitch conditions or weather, and has not been proven to outperform a simple baseline.

Important model caveats

Data ends in 2019 and contains roughly 750 matches. Current teams and conditions may differ. Probabilities can be poorly calibrated, and the original evaluation did not establish a reliable advantage over a baseline. Do not use the output for betting or high-stakes decisions.
